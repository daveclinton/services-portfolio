Built the after-sales module in the main platform. Staff start a complaint from a phone
number or serial number, assign technicians, record troubleshooting, complete
and review job cards, and close or reopen cases. Each case shows its real stage
and the next action.

Took over Mika Care, the older PHP repair system, and fixes problems in live use,
including the serial numbers recorded on replacements, spare-part lists,
approvals and stock received between warehouses. Reviewed a year of repair and
warranty records against the stock and accounting entries behind them, about
30,000 cases, and reported the problems to the finance team with evidence for
each.

At the time of review, the records held about 137,000 repair complaints, 134,000
job cards and 200,000 warranty registrations.
