Shipping mobile apps to Google Play and the App Store: Kotlin/Android warehouse
and warranty applications at Mika, and Flutter ecosystem applications at Tai
Ora.

Android experience includes Jetpack Compose and Material 3 interfaces, Hilt
dependency injection, Navigation Compose, Retrofit/OkHttp/Moshi API clients,
coroutines and StateFlow, encrypted token storage, DataStore, WorkManager,
Firebase Cloud Messaging, geofencing, CameraX, ML Kit barcode scanning, and
Honeywell Data Collection Intent/AIDC hardware integration.

Mobile delivery experience includes Gradle Kotlin DSL, release signing,
collision-safe automated versioning, unit and instrumentation tests, GitHub
Actions, Firebase App Distribution, Google Cloud Workload Identity Federation,
and automated publishing to Google Play closed and production tracks.
