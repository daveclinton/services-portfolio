Building systems that read from SAP HANA and record documents in SAP Business One
through its Service Layer. Experience covers sales orders, deliveries, invoices,
payments, purchase orders, goods receipts and issues, transfers, approvals,
drafts, serial numbers, custom tables and several companies on one server.

Writes are protected with duplicate checks, retries, review of unusual cases and
recovery when SAP does not confirm what it saved. Reads use limited, read-only
queries, and data is kept separate for each company.
