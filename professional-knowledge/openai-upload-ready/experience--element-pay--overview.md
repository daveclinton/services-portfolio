USDC invoicing, collections and payouts, off-ramp from USDC to mobile
money (M-Pesa and local rails), IBAN EUR/USD accounts, and USSD flows
that let customers transact from any phone with no internet. Frontend
work on the marketing site, product surfaces and developer entry points
(blog, documentation, API console, demo).
