Mika is a group of home appliance companies. Its employees use the systems David
builds and supports to serve thousands of customers across Kenya, Uganda and
Rwanda. They spend less time typing the same information twice, can find answers in
company records by asking a question, and can follow a sale, delivery or repair to
the end. The company also has evidence of problems in past records and a handbook
for running its systems.

The SAP environment these systems connect to recorded about 480,000 business
documents across five group companies in the 12 months to November 2026, an
average of about 1,300 a day. These include orders, deliveries, invoices,
payments and stock movements. Not every document passes through the systems
David built.

## How it was built

Built the main back-end system and staff web application, with about 1,600 automated
back-end tests, along with native Kotlin Android applications. Architected and
operated Django and FastAPI services backed by PostgreSQL and Redis, integrated with
SAP Business One Service Layer, SAP HANA, MSSQL, Wialon, Firebase, WhatsApp, SMS,
email and object storage. Built React/Next.js and Vue/Vite interfaces. Deployed the
systems to self-managed Linux infrastructure using Nginx, systemd, Gunicorn, Uvicorn
and Docker Compose, with backups, health checks and automatic return to the previous
code when a release fails.
