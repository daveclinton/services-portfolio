This was David's favorite role. Untapped Global lets investors fund vehicles, solar
equipment and other things that small businesses use to earn money. David joined in
May 2022, the month it launched its digital investment platform.

He worked on the investment platform, which processed investors' transactions and
showed them how their investments were doing. He built the KYC screens and connected
Plaid for payments. He also worked on the internal management platform that the
investment and operations teams used to run the business every day. He connected financial information from other services
and helped bring in new data as it arrived.

Untapped Global reports these company totals from 2021 to the end of January 2025. It
supported 578,231 entrepreneurs, helped them earn $46.5 million in revenue, created
$3.83 of small-business revenue for each $1 invested and displaced 2,243 tons of CO2.
These are the company's results, which David's work contributed to. David worked there
from May 2022 to May 2024.
