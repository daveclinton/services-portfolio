Built showroom and outlet workflows that record sales, payments, deliveries and
goods received in SAP. Built a consignment check for Rwanda that compares stock
counts with serial records to find what a branch has sold, with a person
approving each step.

Built a price sync that copies one SAP price list to the company's stores on
Glovo, an online delivery marketplace, and a test environment for it. Built SMS
campaigns, push messages, email and in-app notices, with failed sends retried and
kept in a record.
