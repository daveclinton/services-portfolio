Full product ecosystem spanning a Django/DRF backend, Flutter mobile app,
Angular administration dashboard, and Next.js/React experiences. The mobile app
was shipped to Apple's App Store and Google Play; backend and administration
services run on AWS-hosted infrastructure; Next.js versions run on Vercel.

The system includes OpenAI conversational features, multi-provider AI support,
S3/CloudFront media delivery, video processing, notifications, social login,
analytics, school and user administration, and content workflows.

The ecosystem has been rolled out to three schools and serves approximately 30
monthly users.

## External services and APIs

- **AI and media generation:** OpenAI, Google Gemini and Luma AI.
- **Cloud and storage:** AWS S3, CloudFront, AWS load balancing, Google Cloud
  Storage and Firebase Admin/Cloud Messaging.
- **Identity:** Google OAuth/Sign-In, Sign in with Apple and Facebook Login.
- **Email:** Mailgun and Brevo transactional delivery.
- **Media ingestion:** YouTube/yt-dlp and ImgBB, alongside FFmpeg/MoviePy
  processing and presigned S3 upload/download flows.
- **Distribution:** Apple App Store, Google Play and Vercel.
