category: engineering

Checking business records against the documents behind them, explaining
differences with evidence and correcting them without overwriting history.
Experience covers stock counts, serial numbers, repair and warranty records,
vehicle trips, loyalty points and orders between companies.

Preview runs, run logs, dead-letter handling and automated checks help teams see
what will change before it is saved. Unclear items are set aside for a person to
decide.
