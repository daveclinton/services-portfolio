## SaaS architecture and modernization

Specialized in building and rebuilding multi-tenant SaaS applications with
real tenant isolation. This includes separating domain, application and
infrastructure concerns through Clean Architecture; implementing
authentication, role-based access control and identity management with
Keycloak, Laravel Sanctum and custom IAM systems; and delivering REST APIs with
OpenAPI documentation.

A recurring modernization pattern is taking an MVP that worked for roughly 50
users but became difficult to operate as it approached 500 users—often because
of missing tenant isolation and an unstructured codebase—and rebuilding it so
the system can scale and another developer can operate it without the founder
being continuously involved.

The wider SaaS stack includes React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Express,
NestJS, Laravel, MERN and PERN architectures, MongoDB, PostgreSQL and Docker.
