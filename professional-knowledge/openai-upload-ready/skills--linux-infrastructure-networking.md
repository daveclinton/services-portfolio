Deploying and operating application services on self-managed Linux systems:
Nginx reverse proxies, systemd services and timers, Gunicorn/Uvicorn processes,
Docker Compose dependencies, UFW firewalls, logs, health checks, backups,
release activation and rollback.

Hands-on networking experience includes internal DNS and virtual hosts, LAN and
DMZ routing, TLS termination boundaries, HTTP forwarding and proxy headers,
CORS/CSRF and trusted-host policy, Unix sockets, TCP/IP connectivity, SSH and
Paramiko, and diagnosing connectivity to ERP, database, telematics, and hardware
systems.
